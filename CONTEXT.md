# Origin Japan

The language for Origin Japan's public site: the cars they sell, and the messages buyers send them.

## Vehicles

**Vehicle**:
One physical car Origin Japan can sell, or has sold.
_Avoid_: Listing, stock item, car (when you mean the record)

**Draft**:
A vehicle Adam has not finished. It is not on the site.
_Avoid_: Unpublished, hidden, in prep

**Availability**:
Whether a public vehicle is in prep, on water, for sale, or sold. In prep and on water mean buyers can see it and it has no public price. A public price means for sale. A draft has no availability.
_Avoid_: Listing status, active, inventory state

**On water**:
A public vehicle that is in transit to the UK. It is in stock, it has no public price, and buyers can send a vehicle enquiry.
_Avoid_: Shipping, in transit (as the status name)

**Stock**:
The public vehicles that are in prep, on water, or for sale. The stock page shows only these. Vehicles for sale with a price are shown before vehicles that are in prep or on water. Buyers see it labelled Showroom, at `/showroom`.
_Avoid_: Current stock, inventory, catalogue (when you mean this set)

**Sold stock**:
The public vehicles that are sold. Same vehicles, shown on their own page. A sold vehicle never appears in stock.
_Avoid_: Archive, previous stock, history

**Make**:
The manufacturer of a vehicle, such as Toyota or Volvo.
_Avoid_: Brand, marque

**Model**:
The name Adam files that vehicle under, including the variant when buyers know it that way, such as Levorg 2.0 GT-S. There is no separate trim.
_Avoid_: Trim, variant, derivative

**Body**:
The shape of a vehicle: Convertible, Pickup, Coupe, MPV, Hatchback, or Estate.
_Avoid_: Style, type

**Transmission**:
How a vehicle changes gear: Manual, Automatic, or DSG.
_Avoid_: Gearbox

**Seller's notes**:
The text Adam writes about a vehicle for buyers to read.
_Avoid_: Description, write-up, advert

## Buying

**Personal import**:
A sourcing service for a car that is not yet a vehicle in Newport.
_Avoid_: Order, commission, sourcing request (as the service name)

**Buyer**:
A person who sends a message about a vehicle or a personal import.
_Avoid_: Customer, client, user, lead (the person)

**Lead**:
A message from a buyer. It is one of three kinds: a contact lead, an import request, or a vehicle enquiry.
_Avoid_: Enquiry (as the umbrella), submission, test drive

**Contact lead**:
A lead from the contact page. It is not tied to one vehicle.
_Avoid_: General enquiry

**Import request**:
A lead about a personal import. It names a make, a model, and a year.
_Avoid_: Wanted ad, brief

**Vehicle enquiry**:
A lead about one vehicle that is in prep, on water, or for sale. A sold vehicle has no vehicle enquiry.
_Avoid_: Test drive, offer, quote

## Reference

**Grade**:
The overall auction grade Adam records on a vehicle, such as 4 or 4.5. It is optional. Buyers can see it on the vehicle page and filter stock by it.
_Avoid_: Score, rating

**Grade sheet**:
An inspector's condition sheet from a Japanese auction. The grade sheets page explains how to read one. A grade sheet is not itself a field on a vehicle.
_Avoid_: Auction report, condition report
