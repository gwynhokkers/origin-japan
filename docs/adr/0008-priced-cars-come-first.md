# Priced for-sale vehicles come first

On the stock page, vehicles for sale with a price are shown before vehicles that are in prep or on water. Adam can set the order inside each of those two groups. If he has not set an order, a group falls back to newest published. A buyer sort by price, year, or mileage reorders inside each group and does not mix the groups. Newest-first across the whole page would bury the cars people can buy today under cars that are still being prepared or still at sea.
