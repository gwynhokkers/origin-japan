# One vehicle catalogue, two public views

Stock and sold stock are views over the same vehicles, split by availability. A public vehicle is in prep, on water, for sale, or sold. Drafts are in neither view. The stock page shows in prep, on water, and for sale, with priced for-sale vehicles before in-prep and on-water vehicles. Sold stock shows sold vehicles only. Sold pages do not show a price. Separate catalogues would drift, which is already happening on the current site, where sold stock still presents itself as cars for sale.
